# Support

Commercial support is available under a separate agreement. Until a support
contract is in place, use the repository issue tracker for bugs and integration
questions.

Suggested support tiers:

| Tier | Response | Includes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Evaluation | best effort | docs, issue tracker |
| Research | 2 business days | integration help, release access |
| Production | 1 business day / incident | priority fixes, upgrade guidance |
| Enterprise | contractual | named contact, security review, custom adapters |

Support does not include trading advice, strategy performance guarantees, or
exchange account management.
